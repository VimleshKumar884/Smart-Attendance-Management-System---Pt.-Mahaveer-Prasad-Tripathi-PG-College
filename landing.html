Never Miss the 75% Mark

Our smart warning system tracks every student's attendance in real time. The moment attendance drops below 75% in any subject — a red alert fires instantly.

  • 🔴 Red warning badge on subject row
  • 🔔 Instant notification to student dashboard
  • 📊 Color coded: Green (75%+), Red (Below)
